# Home screen key art — 3 sizes

Put the images here with these exact names (JPG):

| file | aspect | used on | empty space for the UI |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home-desktop.jpg` | 16:9 (e.g. 1920×1080) | computers, wide screens | the **right 40%** (logo + buttons) |
| `home-tablet.jpg` | 4:3 (e.g. 1600×1200) | tablets, square-ish windows | the **right 40%** |
| `home-mobile.jpg` | 9:16 (e.g. 1080×1920) | phones | the **top 25%** (logo) and **bottom 35%** (buttons) |

The page picks the right one by itself. If a file is missing, the drawn scene is shown instead.

## The heroes (same in all 3 prompts)

> six cute cartoon heroes, each one is a living Arabic letter with big round white eyes and small black legs, smooth glossy candy-like shapes with thick dark outlines:
> a tall straight **blue Alif (ا)** swinging forward like a spear, the hero in front and biggest;
> a **green Nun (ن)** shaped like a bowl with a golden dot above it, jumping and shooting golden dots;
> a **purple Taa (ط)** holding a magic staff with a glowing gem, casting purple sparkles;
> a wide **orange Sad (ص)** charging like a shield bearer;
> a **pink-red Baa (ب)** flying with its dot below it;
> a **magenta Ain (ع)** leaping through the air with a fiery speed trail.

## Shared style

> original cartoon mobile game key art, dynamic action poses, bright saturated colors, clean cel shading, thick outlines, sunny blue sky with soft clouds, snowy Atlas mountains and a Moroccan kasbah on a hill in the background, green grass field in the foreground, epic but cute, high detail, no text, no logo, no letters written anywhere, no watermark
